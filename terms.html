6. Health and safety

Ironstride is not medical advice and is not a medical device.

  • Talk to your doctor before you start a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant, are recovering from an injury, or have not exercised in a while.
  • Stop exercising and get medical help if you feel pain, faintness, dizziness, chest pressure or shortness of breath.
  • Heart rate, effort zones, distance, speed and other numbers are estimates for your own workouts. They can be wrong. Do not use them to diagnose or treat any condition.
  • Goals, streaks and campaign plans are suggestions. Move at your own pace. Rest when you need to.

Outdoor safety.

  • Pay attention to traffic, weather, terrain, wildlife and the people around you.
  • Do not look at or use your phone while you drive, ride a bike in traffic, or cross a road. Never record a "walk" while driving.
  • Tell someone where you are going when you go out alone, and carry water and a light when you need them.
  • Respect private property, closures and local rules.

Routes are suggestions. Routes, loops, campaign roads, place names and maps come from automated services and from OpenStreetMap contributors. They can be wrong, out of date, closed, unsafe or on private land. Always check conditions on the ground and follow posted signs and local laws. You choose where you go, and you are responsible for your own safety.
